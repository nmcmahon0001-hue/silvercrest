Frequently Asked Questions

Click "Get started free" on our homepage, enter your full name, email address, and a password of at least 6 characters. Your account is created instantly and you receive a $1,000 welcome balance to get started.

Your account number is displayed on your main dashboard under the "Account Number" card. You can also find it in the Profile section. Tap the "Copy" button to copy it instantly and share with anyone who needs to send you money.

Go to the Profile section from the sidebar or bottom navigation. Currently, profile viewing is available. For changes to your name or email, please contact us at support@silvercrestfin.com.

Click "Send Money" from the dashboard or sidebar. Enter the recipient's registered email address, the amount you want to send, and an optional note. Click "Send Money" and the transfer happens instantly. Both you and the recipient will see the transaction in your history.

First check that the recipient's email is correct and that they have a SilverCrest account. Also ensure you have sufficient balance. If the problem persists, contact us at support@silvercrestfin.com with the details and we'll resolve it within 24 hours.

You can send up to your available balance in a single transfer. There are no daily limits for verified accounts. Transfers are instant and completely free between SilverCrest users.

Go to "Cards" in the sidebar. You can request a free Virtual Card (instant), a Physical Debit Card ($5, delivered in 3–5 business days), or apply for a Credit Card. Virtual cards are ready immediately for online purchases.

Yes! Go to Cards → "Add External Card". We accept Visa, Mastercard, and Verve cards. Enter your card number, expiry date, CVV, and name. Your card is linked securely using bank-grade encryption.

Go to "Crypto" in the sidebar. Select the coin you want (Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, or BNB), choose "Buy", enter the dollar amount, and confirm. The purchase is deducted from your balance instantly. Note: this is a simulated feature for demonstration purposes.

In the Crypto section, select the coin, choose "Sell", and enter the dollar amount you want to receive. The equivalent crypto will be deducted from your holdings and the cash added to your balance.

Yes. SilverCrest uses 256-bit SSL encryption, the same standard used by major banks worldwide. Your account is protected by Supabase Auth, and Row Level Security ensures you can only ever access your own data. We never store passwords in plain text.

Password reset is coming soon. In the meantime, please email us at support@silvercrestfin.com with your registered email address and we'll help you regain access within 24 hours.

If you notice any unauthorized transactions or suspicious activity, contact us immediately at support@silvercrestfin.com. Include your account email and details of the suspicious activity. We take security incidents very seriously and respond within 1 hour.

Go to "Loans" in the sidebar. Choose your loan type (Personal, Business, or Mortgage), enter the amount, select your repayment period, and review the loan summary. Click "Apply Now" to submit your application. We review and respond within 24 hours.

Personal Loans start at 12% APR, Business Loans at 9% APR, and Mortgages at 6.5% APR. The loan calculator in the app shows your exact monthly payments and total interest before you apply.
